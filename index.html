    Offense Defense Special teams (ST, 5%)

    Offense and defense each get 47.5% of the first wheel and special teams gets 5%. Positions inside a side have even odds. A picked position sits out on that wheel (winner or loser) for the next 10 spins, then comes back.

    Hot Streak Curse: three wins in a row triggers it right after your Win Wheel pick. Pick Your Poison: Double Trouble 47.5% (lose two Loser Wheel picks), Choose Your Demise 47.5% (lose your highest-rated player at two Loser Wheel positions), The Ultimate Sacrifice 5% (lose your highest-overall player). A loss resets the streak.

    Losing Streak Blessing: three losses in a row triggers it right after your Loser Wheel pick. Pick Your Miracle: Double the Luck 47.5% (gain two Win Wheel picks), Upgrade Your Luck 47.5% (take the highest-rated available player at two Win Wheel positions), The Ultimate Handout 5% (take the highest-overall player in the pool). A win resets the streak.
